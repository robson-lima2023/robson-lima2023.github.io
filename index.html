About

I am a forest scientist working at the interface between forest mensuration, Amazonian forest ecology, remote sensing, biodiversity modeling, and quantitative methods applied to tropical forests.

Research Areas

Amazonian Giant Trees

Ecology, distribution, monitoring, and conservation of giant trees in Amazonian forests.

LiDAR Remote Sensing

Mapping forest structure, canopy height, biomass, and landscape-scale patterns using airborne LiDAR.

Species Distribution Modeling

Modeling current and future species distributions under climate and environmental change.

Forest Mensuration

Forest biometrics, growth, yield, structure, volume, biomass, and forest management.

Featured Projects

Current projects include integrated monitoring of Amazonian giant trees, LiDAR-based forest mapping, species distribution modeling, forest diversity mapping, and long-term ecological research in the eastern Amazon.

Teaching

Teaching activities include Forest Mensuration, Forest Biometrics, Tropical Forest Management, Experimental Statistics, Species Distribution Modeling in R, and Multivariate Analysis in Ecology.

Publications

Scientific publications focus on forest ecology, forest mensuration, LiDAR, Amazonian giant trees, biodiversity mapping, and climate change.
